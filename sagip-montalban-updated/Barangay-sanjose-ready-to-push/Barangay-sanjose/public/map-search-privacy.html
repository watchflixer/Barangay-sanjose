Directions / map search: Terms & privacy

Map-search feature information · Updated 1 October 2026

Terms of use

Ang map search at Directions ay pantulong sa pagpili ng lokasyon at pagplano ng biyahe. Hindi ito garantiya ng survey-grade coordinates, kumpletong Google Maps coverage, road access, o kaligtasan ng ruta. Sundin ang aktwal na road conditions, official advisories at emergency responders. Huwag mag-operate ng search habang nagmamaneho.

Ang AREA/APPROX result ay hindi verified house, Blk/Lot o entrance pin. I-check ang address at mapa. Para sa exact user-chosen point, gamitin ang Pin sa mapa o maglagay ng sariling coordinates.

Kung ginagamit mo ang Google Maps places o binubuksan mo ang Google Maps mula rito, kasama sa mga tuntunin ng paggamit ang Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Ang Google place content ay ipinapakita ng official Google Places UI Kit at pinananatili ang Google attribution at disclosures. Hindi kinokopya ang Google database sa lokal na listahan.

Ang ibang results at base map ay mula sa Mapbox at OpenStreetMap providers. OSM data: © OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL. Maaaring magbago, mawalan ng koneksyon, o magkaroon ng kulang na address data ang mga provider.

Privacy information

  • Kapag nag-live search, ipinapadala sa provider ang query at ang gitna ng mapa bilang proximity hint. Maaaring may coordinates ang query na ikaw ang nag-type. Huwag magsama ng confidential/personal information na hindi kailangang hanapin.
  • Normal search uses Mapbox (addresses) and Photon/OSM (places/businesses). Google requests are made only when the administrator has enabled Google UI Kit and you submit a search. Open Google Maps opens a third-party site/app with your query or Maps link.
  • Ang Your location ay gumagamit ng browser/device location permission para sa origin/navigation. Hindi ito kailangang ibigay para lang makapag-type ng destination. Kapag humingi ng ruta, ang origin/destination coordinates ay ipinapadala sa routing provider.
  • Local/OSM places at sariling coordinate pins na pinili mo ay maaaring ma-save sa browser localStorage bilang Recent/Home/Work/Saved. Maaari itong alisin sa browser site-data settings. Short-lived OSM search caching is memory-only.
  • Google UI Kit content/geometry at temporary Mapbox address results are not stored as offline history. Those choices are session-only. A manually selected point can be saved with its numeric coordinate label instead of a temporary provider-derived address.
  • Third-party services receive network information such as your IP address, browser information and website referrer, under their own policies. Google's Privacy Policy is incorporated for the Google-powered features. Review Mapbox's Privacy Policy, Photon's service information and the OSMF Privacy Policy as applicable.

This notice describes this map-search feature, not every other service on the website. For questions or local location-data corrections, contact the website administrator or Barangay San Jose office through the app's contact section.
